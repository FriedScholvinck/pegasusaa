Thoughts on the post-pandemic new normal in air travel

14 January 2021 · Jim Robinson

Pegasus is pleased to share this recently published article in the Journal of Airport Management prepared by our Managing Director, Jim Robinson. This paper explores the impact of these factors and how they could permanently transform the air travel industry, and how the trends of global digitalization present a unique opportunity to reinvent the entire aviation sector business model as well as the end-to-end travel experience.

To achieve this will require meaningful collaboration of the digital technology and air transport sectors to drive innovation and transformational change in response to this challenge for society.

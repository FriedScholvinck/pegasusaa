About us

Pegasus Aviation Advisors, led by industry veterans, has a proven track-record of delivering solutions in the aviation sector. Our team of Subject Matter Experts (SME) each have more than 25 years relevant experience. We offer a hands-on approach, with the ability to provide independent advice and direction, with a clear focus on value addition.

Our expertise includes airport development and operations, airline strategy, government regulations, international financial institutions (IFIs), public private partnerships (PPP), revenue enhancement, financial analysis and more. Our success hinges on our ability to develop a trusted relationship with our clients.

Pegasus Aviation Advisors delivers solutions through offices established in France, Ireland, United States, United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates, Oman and Australia.
